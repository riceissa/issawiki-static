Repetition granularity

From Issawiki (static archive)

Repetition granularity or review granularity is the size of the "unit of review". For example, in Anki the repetition granularity is the card, usually a small card (which usually corresponds to an atomic fact or single-chunk size of info). In spaced proof review, the granularity is the proof. In Duolingo, it is the lesson.
