Deck options for small cards

From Issawiki (static archive)

Here are my deck options (as of 2023-10-30) for my small cards decks, specifically conceptual cards (so not for things like memorizing foreign vocabulary or names of capitals). The order of the options follows Anki ⁨version 2.1.65.

Daily limits:

  • New cards/day: 5
  • Maximum reviews/day: 1000

New Cards:

  • Learning steps: 1d 10d
  • Graduating interval: 25 (days)
  • Easy interval: 30 (days)
  • Insertion order: Sequential (oldest cards first)

Lapses:

  • Relearning steps: 1d
  • Minimum interval: 1 (days)
  • Leech threshold: 4
  • Leech action: Suspend Card

Timer:

  • Maximum answer seconds: 60
  • Show answer timer: no

Burying:

  • Bury new siblings: yes
  • Bury reviews siblings: yes

Audio:

  • Don't play audio automatically: no
  • Skip question when replaying answer: no

Advanced:

  • Maximum interval: 36500 (days)
  • Starting ease: 2.50
  • Easy bonus: 1.30
  • Interval modifier: 1.00
  • Hard interval: 1.20
  • New interval: 0.40
