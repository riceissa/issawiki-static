Category:AI timelines arguments

From Issawiki (static archive)

An AI timelines argument gives a standalone/self-contained account of when humans are expected to create an AGI. In other words, the pages in this category do not simply give evidence for whether we should expect AGI to come earlier or later, but actually produce a numerical estimate or a probability distribution over years.
